# Privacy Policy — coach-sync

Last updated: 30 August 2026

## Who this covers

**coach-sync** is a personal application built and operated by one individual
for their own use. It has a single user: its author. It is not offered to,
marketed to, or usable by anyone else.

## What data is accessed

With the account holder's explicit consent, the application reads the following
from the Google Health API, using read-only scopes:

- Body weight and body fat percentage
- Sleep sessions and their duration
- Daily resting heart rate and heart rate variability
- Exercise sessions (type, duration, heart rate)

## What the application does with it

Data is written to files stored on the author's own personal computer and on a
private home server accessible only over a private network. It is used solely
to produce weekly summaries of the author's own training and body composition.

## What the application does NOT do

- It does not write, modify, or delete any data in the Google account. Every
  scope requested is read-only.
- It does not share, sell, rent, or transfer data to any third party.
- It does not use data for advertising, profiling, or analytics.
- It does not transmit data to any server other than the author's own.
- It has no other users, and therefore no other users' data.

## Retention and deletion

Data is retained on the author's own devices for as long as it is useful, and
can be deleted at any time by deleting the local files.

Access can be revoked at any time from
[Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), which
immediately and permanently ends the application's ability to read any data.
